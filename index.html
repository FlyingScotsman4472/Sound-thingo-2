What to expect

Real recordings sound like engines, but their original rev changes are baked into the clip. The throttle changes the recording's playback speed and pitch. It is not a fully RPM-synchronised, multi-sample engine model. Precise matching to your Subaru would require reading OBD2 data with extra hardware. Audio streaming requires internet access. This is a prototype and the recordings do not track real engine RPM.

For parked testing or passenger operation only. NSW learner and P-plate phone rules prohibit drivers from operating a phone while driving, including for Bluetooth audio.
